The story.

Half the agencies in this industry sell reports. Pretty PDFs full of charts the client doesn't read, full of work that doesn't move the business forward. The other half sell websites and call them marketing.

We got tired of seeing service businesses pay $3,000 a month for nothing. So we built Foundbly around one promise. Show up when people search. Get cited when they ask AI. Bring in leads.

That's it. That's the whole company.

We're small on purpose. Small means we know every client by name. Small means the person who built your strategy is the person doing the work. Small means we say no to people we can't help.

We've built sites for hearing clinics, kitchen manufacturers, and ADA compliance firms. We've gotten clients from invisible to page one in 60 days. We've gotten others mentioned in ChatGPT answers their competitors don't even know they're missing from.

The thing we care about most is that you can call us in six months and have a real answer to one question. Are you getting more leads than you were before Foundbly?
